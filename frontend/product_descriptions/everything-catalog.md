### Everything OS

Everything OS is the world's first contextual operating system. Instead of navigating through apps, menus, and windows, users describe what they want to achieve, and the system understands intent, gathers context, and orchestrates actions automatically.

Examples: "Build me a website for my store." "Summarize my emails and schedule meetings." "Find the place from this photo and send directions." "Book a trip for next weekend."

Core principles: No apps, only actions. No tabs, only flow. No borders, only possibilities.

### Personal AI Core

- Understands routines and preferences
- Maintains project and document context across sessions
- Performs multi-step tasks autonomously
- Predicts needs and surfaces useful information before being asked
- Works entirely on-device whenever possible
- Evolves with usage over time

### Experience

Everything X is designed to replace the traditional idea of a smartphone. It combines flagship hardware, contextual intelligence, and a new operating paradigm into a single device that feels less like a phone and more like a personal computing companion built for the future.
